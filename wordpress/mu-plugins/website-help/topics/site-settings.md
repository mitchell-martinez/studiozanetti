# Site settings, footer, and public business details

Open **Site Settings** in the WordPress sidebar. These values are shared and can affect the header, footer, search/social metadata, image metadata defaults, and structured data across many pages. Confirm the intended site-wide effect before saving.

## Basic identity and footer values

- **Site Name** is the public brand name and may appear in the header and structured data.
- **Tagline** is shared supporting brand text.
- **Copyright Text** can be left blank to use the current year and site name automatically.
- **Social Links** need both an accurate platform label and the official public profile URL. Remove obsolete profiles rather than leaving broken destinations.

## Business Entity

Use only verified public facts: description, public email and phone, logo, representative image, public address, coordinates, service areas, broad price range, founding date, awards, and authoritative identity URLs. Keep business name, address, and contact details consistent with trusted external listings. Do not enter private addresses, internal pricing notes, unverified awards, or guessed coordinates.

Business image licensing, acquisition-page, credit, and copyright values act as defaults for eligible images without attachment-specific overrides. Enter only truthful rights information.

## Primary Photographer

Enable **Publish Person Entity** only when the named person should be publicly identified as the primary photographer. Confirm the public name, owner/employee relationship, job title, biography, profile image, About page, identity profiles, expertise, and awards. Disabling publication is safer than publishing incomplete or unwanted personal information.

## Service Catalog

The catalog is the authoritative list used by page Primary Service, Services Grid cards, Pricing Packages, and structured-data relationships. Each service has a stable lowercase key containing letters, numbers, and hyphens. Do not change an established key casually because linked identifiers depend on it. Keep service name, type, description, canonical page, representative image, and service areas aligned with visible offerings.

## After saving

Check the homepage, footer, a service page, a representative image/page preview, and any affected SEO audit findings. Blank optional entity fields are omitted rather than guessed. Site Settings can change values exposed by the existing frontend; they cannot redesign the footer or invent a new schema relationship.

Website Help cannot change global settings or expose private configuration. Contact the webmaster before changing stable service keys, identity relationships, legal/licensing information, or site-wide layout behavior, and whenever an expected shared change does not appear after a normal refresh.
