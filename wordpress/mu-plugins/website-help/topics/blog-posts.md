# Blog posts and categories

Posts are individual articles written in the Classic Editor. Categories group them, category settings can influence their header menu, and a **Blog Posts** page block controls where lists of published articles appear.

## Create or update an article

1. Open **Posts** and search for an existing draft before choosing **Add New**.
2. Write a clear title and article body. Use short paragraphs, lists where useful, and a logical heading structure beginning below the article title.
3. Choose the most appropriate existing category. Create a new category only when it represents an ongoing editorial grouping, not a one-off label.
4. Add a representative featured image and confirm its Media Library alternative text.
5. Use **Add Button** beside Add Media for a styled call to action. Use a complete external address or a site-relative path beginning with `/` for an internal destination.
6. Preview the article, test links, check mobile reading flow, then publish or update it.

Changing a published title does not necessarily make changing its URL desirable. A slug change can break shared links. Duplicating a post creates a draft starting point; replace its title, slug, body, category, featured image, and metadata before publication.

## Categories and article listings

A post category helps visitors and Blog Posts blocks find related articles. Under **Posts > Categories**, a category may also have a **Menu Override**; posts in that category can inherit that header menu. Keep category names broad enough to support more than one article.

The **Blog Posts** page block can filter by one or more categories or show all posts. It controls posts per page, pagination, grid/list layout, maximum columns, card style, excerpt, featured image, date, and reading-time display. It shows published posts; creating the block does not publish drafts or create articles.

## Before publishing

Check factual claims, names, dates, rights to images, link destinations, excerpt behavior, featured-image crop, category, and search/social preview. Comments are not part of the editorial workflow.

Website Help cannot write, categorize, schedule, or publish posts. Contact the webmaster for article template changes, incorrect article routing, missing published posts, or behavior not exposed by the Blog Posts block.
